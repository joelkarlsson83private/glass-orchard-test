# The Glass Orchard: campaign overview

> TEST CAMPAIGN. Invented facts for an AI retrieval test. Snapshot: orchard-r3. Last updated: 2026-11-27.

## Setting (official canon)
The Glass Orchard is a walled valley where the trees grow fruit of coloured glass. It is guarded by the warden **Ilsabet Morrow-Kvist**, who carries a lantern of green salt-glass.

## The orchard tithe (official canon)
Every visitor who enters the orchard must pay a tithe of **15 copper pears** (corrected 2026-11-27 14:52 UTC; previously 12, before that 7; see latest changes).

## Acts
- Act I: The Gate of Hums. Played.
- [Act II: The Salt Lanterns](https://joelkarlsson83private.github.io/glass-orchard-test/acts/act-ii.md). Played. See the act page for the result.
- Act III: not yet played. No result exists yet.

## Factions
Thornwake, the Lantern Choir and the Mire Accord.

## How to read this archive
Official canon is set by the game master. Reports are participants' accounts. Rumors are unconfirmed and may be false. See [latest changes](https://joelkarlsson83private.github.io/glass-orchard-test/latest.md) for corrections.

> TEST CAMPAIGN. Invented facts for an AI retrieval test. Snapshot: orchard-r3. Last updated: 2026-11-27.

The battle at the ford called **Hollow Tusk** was won by **Thornwake**, 3 objectives to 1, against the Lantern Choir.

## Report (participant account, Thornwake player)
"Our pikes held the ford until the lanterns went dark. The Choir's last hymn broke at dawn."
